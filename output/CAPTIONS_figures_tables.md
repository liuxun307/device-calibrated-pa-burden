## Fig 1. Study design and three-module analytical workflow

**Files / 文件**：`figures/panel/Fig1_study_design.pdf`；`figures/combined/Fig1.pdf`

**English.** Schematic of the study design and the three analytical modules. Module 1 (device-measured anchor): NHANES 2011-2014 accelerometry with mortality linkage is used to fit survey-weighted Cox proportional-hazards models with restricted cubic splines, yielding both a device-scale relative-risk curve and an individual-level calibration that converts self-reported activity onto the device scale. The models rest on two nested samples: first, the device sample of 7,854 adults aged 25 years or older described in Table 1, of whom 7,842 had linked mortality follow-up and 769 died; second, after additionally requiring complete covariates, the 7,007 adults analysed in the Cox models, in whom 664 deaths occurred. Module 2 (pooled external evidence): 5 external cohort studies (21 cohort-specific exposure contrasts) covering hip, wrist and self-reported measurement are harmonised by meta-regression with measurement method as a covariate, yielding a pooled device-scale relative-risk curve intended to be transportable across populations.
